CONSOLIDATING ANALYSIS

Aurora’s analysis environment enters its final consolidation stage, bringing its individual observation systems toward a stable and coherent analytical foundation for the next stage of development.

AUGUST 2026

EXPANDING ANALYSIS

Aurora’s analysis environment expands into a broader set of dedicated observation and visualization systems. Spectral, temporal, phase, spatial and signal-behaviour analysis progressively converge toward a coherent analytical platform.

Aurora’s fundamental signal-processing architecture begins taking shape. Core processing concepts, validation methods and the foundations of its analysis environment are developed and progressively consolidated.
